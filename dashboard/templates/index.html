Motion is enabled on this device. robot-stack/SAFETY.md applies: a clear area, an operator on the controller abort with the e-stop in hand, tether slack checked, and adequate battery. Every command here is bounded in time and stops itself.

Motion

Each press commands a velocity for a fixed time and then stops. The result reports what the robot measured, not what it was told — the delivered fraction is the number that explains a robot that looks like it is not moving.
